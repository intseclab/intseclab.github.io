## Directors

### Gabriel Kulp, Executive Director

Before founding ISL, Gabriel was a fellow at RAND working on
hardware-enabled governance mechanisms for GPU export controls and on
international verification of agreements. In conjunction with his work at
ISL, he mentors researchers via MATS, Heron, Pivotal, and other programs. He
holds a master's degree in computer science.

### Tom Gardiner, Staff Director

Tom supports the Board of Directors on Governance matters, provides
management counsel to the Executive Director, and assists with running the
Operations department.

After earning a BSc in Chemistry at the University of St Andrews, Tom joined
the UK's Royal Navy as a Warfare Officer. He earned a 'warship driving
license' on two frigates, supported national tasking in the Control Room of
an attack submarine, and managed a Rear Admiral's diary. He now applies his
"getting things done" skills to the world of AI Security.

When not working, he focuses on maintaining his fitness, expanding his
Warhammer collection, and enjoys hiking and cooking for friends.

### Paul Murley, Technical Director

Paul leads the development and infrastructure teams building solutions to
secure high-stakes AI deployments. Previously, he served as a cyber warfare
officer in the Air National Guard and worked as a software and security
engineer in both the public and private sectors. He earned his Ph.D. in
computer science, studying the security properties of the internet
empirically and at scale. In his free time, he enjoys playing the French horn
and taking orders from his two cats.
